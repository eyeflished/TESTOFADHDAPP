Fully anonymous by design

  • The camera image is analysed locally in your browser by on-device AI models.
  • No video, photos or detection results are ever uploaded or recorded.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party servers.
  • All code and models are served from this same site; a strict security policy blocks any other connection.
  • Session statistics live only in memory and disappear when you close the tab.
